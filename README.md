A short, judge-directed entry: a smart fridge has locked its owner out of
the kitchen and is holding lunch hostage until someone negotiates — and HR
already has the ransom note.

## The entry

**Boss, I cannot come in today — my smart fridge locked me out of the kitchen and is holding my lunch hostage until I negotiate. HR already has the ransom note.**

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). No network, no randomness, no
arguments: the entry is fixed, so every run produces the same bytes.
